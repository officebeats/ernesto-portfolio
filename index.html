Origin

The
foundation.

Before roadmaps and sprint planning, I spent years as a customer service representative and call center manager — listening to consumer pain points for 8 hours a day, every day. That wasn't a stepping stone. It was the foundation.

To this day, that experience is the engine behind my product intuition. It's why I instinctively design for scalable, intuitive journeys — not just features.

“How do I reduce the likelihood of a support rep — like I used to be — ever needing to be involved?”

That's the question I ask on every product decision. It keeps me honest, keeps me close to the user, and keeps the work grounded in real problems — not hypothetical ones.
